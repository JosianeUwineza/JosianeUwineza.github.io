About Me

I am a Data Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer with experience in deep learning and medical imaging. My work focuses on developing artificial intelligence systems that can support medical diagnosis, particularly in ultrasound and CT imaging.

My research interests include medical image analysis, deep learning, domain generalization, and the development of reliable AI systems for healthcare applications in resource-limited settings.

Projects

Clinical Validation of Fetal Biometry Models

Tested fetal ultrasound AI models on a clinical trial dataset collected in Kigali. By reviewing the annotations carefully, I fixed a plane recognition problem and raised abdominal circumference plane accuracy from 0.41 to 0.80.

3D Ultrasound and CT Registration

Built a pipeline that aligns 3D ultrasound and CT scans using blood vessel centerlines, with Go-ICP and RANSAC, visualized in Open3D and PyVista.

Annotation Review Tool for 3D Slicer

Leading development of a 3D Slicer extension that lets a second expert review segmentations directly on the images, replacing a manual spreadsheet workflow.

Anatomical 3D Segmentation and Landmark Detection

Used nnU-Net to segment the liver, kidneys and vessels, and to detect kidney landmarks in CT and ultrasound.

Active Learning for Fetal Ultrasound

Cut manual annotation work by 60% while keeping the same accuracy.

Liver Mass Detection in Ultrasound

Built a detection pipeline (DETR, YOLOv5, VarifocalNet) to tell solid from cystic liver masses.
